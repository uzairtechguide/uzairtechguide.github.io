Easy Step-by-Step Installation Guide

STEP 1: Turn on HEN on your PS3

First, boot up your PlayStation 3 console. Look on your home screen (XMB) under the Game column, find the icon that says Enable HEN, and click it. This prepares your console to accept and install unofficial apps and packages that Sony doesn't normally allow.

STEP 2: Find and write down your PS3's IP address

Now go to the Settings column on your PS3 home screen, scroll down to Network Settings, and click on Settings and Status List. Look through the list until you find a row of numbers called the IP Address. Write these numbers down completely.

What it looks like: In our example video, the number looks like 192.168.1.10, but your home router will probably give you a slightly different number sequence.

STEP 3: Launch the multiMan (mmCM) app

Go back to the Game column on your PS3 screen and open up multiMan (which might also be called mmCM on your system). If you use a different file tool like IRISMAN or webmanMOD, that's totally fine too, but this guide will show you how to do it using multiMan because it is the easiest app for beginners.

STEP 4: Turn on FTP and disable the sleep timer

Once multiMan opens up, it automatically starts a background file sharing connection called FTP. Go into the multiMan settings menu and make sure the connection timeout timer is turned off. This keeps your connection active so it doesn't accidentally drop or stop while we send the file over.

STEP 5: Open CX File Explorer on your phone

Put your PS3 controller down for a moment and grab your phone. Open up the CX File Explorer app. Click on the tab that says "Network", tap the button to add a new network location, and pick the standard FTP option from the menu list.

STEP 6: Link your phone straight to your PS3

Now, type in those exact IP address numbers that you wrote down from your PS3 back in Step 2. Leave the user name and password boxes empty and just hit connect. If both devices are on the same Wi-Fi, your phone will open up a secret window showing all the folders inside your PS3!

STEP 7: Go to the packages folder on your PS3 drive

Inside the PS3 storage window on your phone, click on the main internal hard drive partition folder. Navigate through the directory list until you see a folder named packages. Tap to open it. The exact folder path looks like this:

You must put the YouTube file inside this exact folder. If you paste it anywhere else, your PS3 home screen installer will not be able to find the app file.

STEP 8: Copy the YouTube file from your phone to your PS3

Switch over to your phone's normal internal download folder. Find the YouTube NoPSN installation file you downloaded earlier. Hold your finger down on the file icon, select copy, go back into that network packages folder we opened in Step 7, and tap paste. Wait for the transfer bar to reach 100% without shutting your phone screen off.

Important Tip: If your phone screen locks or turns off during the copy process, the file might get corrupted. Keep the screen active until it finishes!

STEP 9: Exit back to the main PS3 home screen

Once your phone shows that the file transfer is completely done, you can close the phone app. Grab your PS3 controller, press the PlayStation button, and exit out of multiMan. This will bring you back to your regular console home menu screen.

STEP 10: Open the Package Manager menu list

On your PS3 Game column menu, scroll down until you spot a folder named Package Manager or Install Package Files. Click into it, and select the internal system storage option where our transferred file is waiting.

STEP 11: Install the YouTube application

You will now see your downloaded YouTube file listed on the screen. Click on it once with the action key. A progress screen will pop up on your TV and start unpacking the application data files. Just wait a minute until it says installation complete.

STEP 12: Launch the app and enjoy your videos

Go back to your main Game column menu, scroll down to the TV/Video Services section, and you will see the new YouTube icon! Click it to launch the app. It will skip past all the annoying login screens and take you straight to the video wall where you can start watching.

HOW TO FIX BLACK SCREEN ERROR: The Internet Clock Fix

If you open the YouTube app and it just hangs on a black screen or won't load anything, do not worry! Exit back to the home screen. Go to the Settings column, click on Date & Time Settings, choose Date and Time, and select Set via Internet. This syncs your PS3's internal clock with the real world, which fixes the app loading errors immediately!

Common Mistakes to Avoid

If you run into any trouble, double-check that you didn't accidentally make one of these simple mistakes:

  • Typing a wrong or misspelled number inside your phone's network configuration box.
  • Forgetting to keep multiMan running on your TV while trying to connect with your phone.
  • Letting your phone go to sleep or lose Wi-Fi connection while the file is transferring.
  • Pasting the YouTube app file into a random folder instead of the specific packages directory.
  • Trying to launch the application before updating your console's date and time via the internet.

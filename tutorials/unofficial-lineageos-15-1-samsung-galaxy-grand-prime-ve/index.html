What is LineageOS 15.1?

LineageOS is a free and open-source operating system for smartphones and tablets, built directly on top of the Android Open Source Project (AOSP). It is the spiritual successor to the legendary CyanogenMod project, which shut down in December 2016 after reaching tens of millions of installs worldwide. When CyanogenMod ended, the developer community forked the project and continued it under the name LineageOS — and today it supports hundreds of different Android devices that manufacturers stopped updating long ago.

LineageOS 15.1 is specifically based on Android 8.1 Oreo. Compared to the factory Android version that shipped with the Grand Prime VE, you get a dramatically cleaner system with zero manufacturer bloatware, much smarter RAM and CPU management that makes the device feel genuinely faster, a built-in Privacy Guard system that lets you control exactly what permissions each app is allowed to use, live display color temperature adjustment for comfortable night-time reading, stronger battery life through aggressive background process control, and monthly security patches from the Android security bulletin that Samsung stopped applying years ago.

Full Step-by-Step Installation Guide

STEP 1: Back Up All Your Important Data

Before you touch a single setting, back up everything you care about. A custom ROM flash requires a complete data wipe that permanently erases everything stored on your phone. Go to Settings, find Backup and Reset or Accounts, and sync your contacts to your Google account or export them to your SIM card. Copy every photo and video off the device onto a computer or cloud storage service. If you have important messages, use a dedicated SMS backup app from the Play Store to export them before proceeding.

No going back: Once the wipe step begins, all your personal data on the device is gone permanently. There is no undo button. Make absolutely sure your backup is complete and safe before moving on to Step 2.

STEP 2: Download the LineageOS 15.1 ROM File

Scroll down to the download section near the bottom of this page and grab the LineageOS 15.1 ZIP file from any of our three verified mirror links. Save it to your computer or directly to the root of your microSD card. Do not rename the file and do not extract or unzip it — TWRP reads and installs the raw ZIP archive directly as downloaded.

File check: The file should end in .zip and its name will typically start with lineage-15.1 followed by a date stamp and device code. If what you downloaded looks different, something may have gone wrong — try a different mirror link.

STEP 3: Copy the Files to Your SD Card or Internal Storage

Transfer the LineageOS 15.1 ZIP file — and your GApps ZIP if you downloaded one — onto the root of your microSD card or into the main folder of your device's internal storage. Keep both files somewhere easy to find, not buried inside subfolders. The simpler the location, the faster and easier it is to select them through TWRP's file browser during the actual flashing steps.

STEP 4: Boot Your Device into TWRP Recovery

Power your Samsung Galaxy Grand Prime VE completely off. Once the screen goes fully dark, hold down the Volume Up button, the Home button, and the Power button all at exactly the same time. Keep all three held together for several seconds until the Samsung logo flashes on screen, then release everything. Your device should boot straight into the blue TWRP recovery interface instead of loading your regular Android home screen.

Tip: If your phone boots back into Android normally, TWRP may not be properly installed yet. You will need to flash TWRP for the Grand Prime VE first before attempting a ROM installation.

STEP 5: Perform a Full Advanced Wipe (Critical Step)

Once inside TWRP, tap the Wipe button on the main menu. Then tap Advanced Wipe so you can choose individual partitions. You must check and select all four of these: Dalvik / ART Cache, Cache, System, and Data. After ticking all four boxes, swipe the confirmation slider at the bottom of the screen all the way to the right to start the wipe. This step is not optional — failing to wipe completely or wiping the wrong partitions is the single most common cause of bootloops and failed installs.

STEP 6: Flash the LineageOS 15.1 ROM ZIP

After the wipe completes, go back to the TWRP main menu and tap Install. Use the file browser to navigate to the LineageOS 15.1 ZIP file you transferred earlier. Tap on it once to select it. A confirmation screen will appear — swipe the slider at the bottom all the way to the right to begin flashing. The process usually takes between 3 to 7 minutes depending on your microSD card speed. Watch the progress log on screen and wait until you see a success confirmation message before doing anything else.

STEP 7: Flash GApps Package (Optional but Recommended)

If you want Google Play Store and the standard Google applications (Gmail, Maps, YouTube, Drive, etc.) on your device, you need to flash a GApps package immediately after flashing the LineageOS ROM — before rebooting the system for the first time. Tap Install again in TWRP, find your GApps ZIP, and swipe to flash it. Make sure the package is built for ARM architecture and is compatible with Android 8.1. Using the wrong GApps version is a common cause of bootloops, so double-check the version label before flashing.

Prefer Google-free? You can skip this step entirely and use F-Droid — a fully open-source app store — after first boot instead. LineageOS works perfectly without any Google services installed and many privacy-conscious users prefer it this way.

STEP 8: Wipe Cache and Dalvik One Final Time

After flashing the ROM and optionally the GApps package, go back to the Wipe section in TWRP one last time and wipe just Cache and Dalvik / ART Cache again. This quick final wipe clears any leftover installation artifacts and tends to produce a noticeably cleaner, glitch-free first boot. It takes less than 30 seconds and is strongly recommended before rebooting.

STEP 9: Reboot Into Your New System

Everything is flashed and ready. From the TWRP main menu tap Reboot, then select System. Your device will power off and begin booting into the brand new LineageOS 15.1 installation. The very first boot always takes significantly longer than a normal startup — anywhere from 3 to 8 minutes — because Android needs to compile all app libraries fresh for your processor. Do not panic, do not force-restart, and do not assume it is frozen. Just put the phone down and wait patiently until the setup wizard appears.

STEP 10: Complete the LineageOS Setup Wizard

Once the Android boot animation ends and the setup screen appears, walk through the initial configuration wizard. Choose your language, connect to your Wi-Fi network, and sign into your Google account if you flashed GApps. LineageOS offers notably more granular privacy options than stock Samsung firmware during setup — take a moment to read each screen and configure your privacy preferences exactly the way you want them from the very start.

STEP 11: Verify the Installation and Enjoy

Once setup is finished and you are at the home screen, go to Settings → About Phone to confirm everything installed correctly. You should see Android version 8.1.0 and the LineageOS build information displayed there. If those details are showing, congratulations — your Samsung Galaxy Grand Prime VE is now running a clean, modern, bloatware-free version of Android that Samsung never officially provided for this device. Enjoy your revived phone!

Common Problems and How to Fix Them

If you run into any trouble during or after installation, check the list below for the most common issues and their straightforward solutions:

  • Phone stuck in a bootloop: You most likely did not wipe all four partitions in Step 5. Re-enter TWRP by holding the same key combo, perform the full Advanced Wipe again on all four partitions, and reflash the ROM from the beginning.
  • TWRP shows "Error 7" during flash: Your installed version of TWRP may be incompatible with this ROM build. Make sure you have the latest TWRP build specific to the Grand Prime VE installed before trying again.
  • Phone will not boot into TWRP: Try the key combination again — hold Volume Up + Home + Power all three together and keep them held for at least 5 full seconds after the Samsung logo appears before releasing any of them.
  • Google Play Store missing after setup: You either forgot to flash GApps in Step 7, or you flashed it in the wrong order. Re-enter TWRP, wipe Cache and Dalvik, flash the GApps package, and reboot again.
  • Wi-Fi will not connect after install: Go to Settings → Date & Time and make sure your date and time are correct. An incorrect system date prevents proper Wi-Fi authentication on many networks.
  • App crashes or random freezes on first day: This is usually caused by residual cache from the old system. Go to Settings → Storage and clear the system cache partition, or simply re-enter TWRP and wipe Cache and Dalvik one more time, then reboot.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Will flashing LineageOS erase all my data?

Yes, completely. The full Advanced Wipe in TWRP permanently deletes everything stored on your device — photos, contacts, apps, messages, and all personal files. Back up absolutely everything important before you start Step 1.

Is LineageOS 15.1 stable enough for daily use on the Grand Prime VE?

For most everyday tasks — calling, messaging, browsing, and casual app use — it is very usable. As with any unofficial port, some minor hardware features may not function perfectly. Check the original XDA forum thread linked above for a detailed up-to-date list of working features and known issues specific to this build.

Can I return to the original Samsung firmware after flashing LineageOS?

Yes. You can always restore stock Samsung firmware using Samsung's official Odin tool on a Windows PC. Download the correct stock firmware ZIP for your exact model number and region, then use Odin in Download Mode to flash it back. Your device will return to factory Samsung software.

Do I need to root the device after installing LineageOS?

LineageOS 15.1 does not include root access by default. If you want root, you can flash the official LineageOS SU add-on package (addonsu) through TWRP right after flashing the ROM. Many users prefer Magisk instead, which provides a systemless root experience that is better for banking apps and SafetyNet.

Which exact model numbers are supported by this ROM?

This ROM targets the Samsung Galaxy Grand Prime VE. The verified compatible model numbers are SM-G531F, SM-G531H, and SM-G531BT. If your device carries a different model number, please confirm compatibility on the original XDA thread before flashing to avoid potential issues.
